## 12. Reference search plan
- Structure fit: a local home-service site whose homepage answers "do you serve my town, roughly what does it cost, how do I book" near the top, with a strong primary action plus a phone alternative. It must show these page types: a single long services page made of repeated service sections, each with its own call to action and a "what we take" list; a booking or quote flow, ideally multi-step with a date picker or photo upload; a reviews page; an information page like Donate; an FAQ accordion; contact with a map and hours. It must have a convincing mobile layout with Book and Call always within thumb reach. A before-and-after or proof section and a service-area section with a town list are strong bonuses.
- Media fit: type-and-colour-led, with phone photos as proof. The client's media will be imperfect phone photos of real jobs, possibly few at first, and no stock, video or illustration [stated, proposal; inferred for volume]. The reference must look finished on bold type, colour blocks and a few honest photos. Reject any reference whose impact depends on cinematic photography, full-bleed video or a custom illustration system.
- Mood words: bold, clean, quick, dependable, local.
- In-niche keywords: junk removal website, junk hauling website, junk removal booking, garage cleanout service, basement cleanout, furniture and appliance removal, mattress disposal, yard waste removal, junk removal and demolition, residential cleanout company.
- Synonyms for the niche: junk hauling, rubbish removal, haul-away service, cleanout service, debris removal, trash hauling, bulk item pickup.
- Adjacent businesses (same world, different business): local moving companies, dumpster and roll-off rental, professional organizers and declutter services, landscaping and yard clean-up crews, estate sale companies.
- Cross-niche analogies (world, and why):
  1. Same-day courier and local delivery services. Speed is the product, booking a slot is the conversion, and the name "Quick Haul" makes the same promise.
  2. Modern direct-to-consumer movers. The same job shape: a crew and a truck at your home, priced by volume, booked by date, and trust has to be earned before they walk in.
  3. Workwear and hardware brands. Bold, plain, hard-wearing type and colour that reads as honest manual work, not polish. A good match for "bold and modern but still clean".
  4. Car detailing and pressure-washing booking sites. The before-and-after transformation is the proof, there are tiered prices, and you pick a slot.
  5. Thrift, reuse and circular-economy shops. The Donate story of giving things a second life matches the audience's interest in "donating/getting rid of unused/unwanted items".
- Technique words: sticky mobile action bar, click-to-call button, oversized display type, colour blocking, truck-load price graphic, before-and-after comparison slider, multi-step booking form, photo upload step, service-area map with town list, review wall with star ratings and source links, "what we take / what we don't" checklist, accordion FAQ, frames and captions that make phone photos look good.
- Exclusions: Quick Haul's own pages (the future domain, plus the Facebook, TikTok and Instagram accounts, URLs not yet known); easyscrapofficial.com and junkteens.com (client-named, same market); any junk removal or demolition business serving MA or NH, including local branches of national franchises; everything in evals/sealed.md.
