Privacy policy

What we collect

When you book or ask for a price, we collect your name, phone number, email, the pickup address, details about the job and any photos you upload. When you visit the site, we collect basic analytics like which pages are viewed. Analytics tools can use cookies to do this, and you can block cookies in your browser settings.

Why we collect it

To confirm and carry out your booking, to give you a price, to send booking confirmations and reminders, and to understand how people use the site so we can improve it.

Photos you send

We use them to price and plan your job. We don't put them on the site or on social media unless you say it's OK.

Text messages

If you check the text message box on the booking form, we text you booking updates and a reminder. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site, the booking calendar, email, text messages and analytics. We don't sell your details.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it for your booking and our records, then we delete it.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time.
