Still good? Someone wants it

A lot of what we pick up still works, so the good stuff goes to local charities and reuse stores instead of the dump.

{{> truck-banner-donate}}

What we can donate

The dresser that doesn't fit the new place, the kids' bikes, the spare couch from the basement. If you'd use it, someone else probably will too.

{{picture name="dresser-on-street" class="o-donateBand__img" alt="A small wooden dresser set out on an empty street, in front of a wooden fence and rooftops" sizes="(min-width: 1025px) 46vw, 100vw"}}

Yes to furniture in good shape, working appliances, kitchenware, clothes and shoes, books, toys, bikes, tools and sports gear.

Not these

  • Anything broken, stained or missing parts
  • Worn-out mattresses
  • Car seats
  • Recalled cribs
  • Anything that's been in a flood or fire
  • Where it goes Local charities and reuse stores in Massachusetts and New Hampshire. We check what they're taking before each drop-off.
  • How to ask Add a note when you book saying what's still good, or tell us when we get there. We'll load it separately.
