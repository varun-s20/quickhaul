# Quick Haul: shortlist
Scout, 2026-11-03. 38 sites long-listed, 35 screened, 6 passed. The full cards are in cards.md, and the picker is moodboard.html.

The honest headline: this is a thin field. Junk removal sites outside MA and NH are almost all franchise or page-builder templates. No in-niche site passed. The six finalists are one strong visual system, two sites with the right conversion structure but ordinary design, and three section donors. That is fewer than the 8 to 10 the procedure asks for. I chose not to pad the list with killed sites.

## Top 3 primary candidates

### 1. FREITAG (https://www.freitag.ch)
The visual system: a hairline-ruled grid, oversized grotesque caps, small mono fact labels, grey pill links with arrows, and a header of pills on the left with a boxed logo in the centre. It is type-led, so phone photos look finished inside its ruled frames. It has no booking structure, so pair it with donors 1 and 2 below.

### 2. Muval (https://www.muval.com.au)
The structure: the phone sits next to "Get instant quotes" in the nav and the mobile drawer. A "What are you moving?" selector leads to "Get prices", and per-city price cards follow. It is the closest match to "where, roughly what cost, how to book". The design is competent but generic, and every image must be swapped.

### 3. Piece of Cake Moving (https://mypieceofcakemove.com)
The local-crew first screen: "Get quote" plus "Or get a call from us" side by side, the phone with hours in the header, a review-score row, and a date-first booking form at 390. Below the hero it is an Avada theme with several tells.

## Why number 1 is number 1
Varun's method needs one reference whose system can carry the whole site in the client's brand. Only FREITAG has a system strong enough to be worth rebuilding. Muval and Piece of Cake have the right conversion parts, but you could swap their brands without changing the design, which is the rubric's 5 to 6 band. FREITAG's impact comes from rules, scale and restraint, not from photography or colour, so it suits a client with a chrome-and-slate logo and only phone photos to come. It also gives Quick Haul a concept from its own world: the page as a load ticket or dispatch sheet, with mono labels carrying real facts (towns, truck-load sizes, "from" prices). It already shares Varun's header and pill-button moves. The cost is that the booking structure has to be built from donors (Muval's selector, Piece of Cake's quote-plus-call). Its fonts are commercial, so the Art Director needs a licensed or free stand-in. If Varun would rather start from a reference that already has the booking structure, Muval is the safer pick, but it will need much more design invention to clear originality 8.

## Section donors (up to 5)
1. Muval: the "What are you moving?" selector, rebuilt as "How much junk?" (an eighth, a quarter, half, full truck) leading to the price. Its per-city price cards become a type-only town list with "from" prices. The mobile drawer pins the phone and the quote button at the bottom.
2. Piece of Cake Moving: the hero block of "Get quote" plus "Or get a call from us" (Shawn's exact ask), the header phone with hours, and the review-score row (Facebook only, linked to verify).
3. Truewerk: the halftone dot band as a section divider, plus a hazard-stripe edge used once, in slate and silver from the logo, not Truewerk's lime.
4. Furniture Bank: the Donate page. Open with the "removal you'd expect, impact you wouldn't" two-part promise in Shawn's voice. Add an impact-numbers block with a pickup button (placeholder figures, must-confirm).
5. Stuf Storage: the hero. One colour field with a town search and one full-width button, the phone icon kept in the mobile header, and "Can't see your town? Call us" under it.
