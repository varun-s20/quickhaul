## Screening totals
38 listed. 35 screened, 3 not screened (Bellhop timed out twice; Kaiyo and Second Use sit behind Cloudflare walls, and I did not work around them). 6 passed: Piece of Cake Moving, Muval, Truewerk, Furniture Bank, FREITAG, Stuf Storage. 29 killed. By channel, passes were N 2 (Piece of Cake, Stuf), G 0, X 4 (Muval, Truewerk, Furniture Bank, FREITAG).

The in-niche field is the problem. Junk removal sites outside MA and NH are almost all franchise or page-builder templates with two or more anti-slop tells. Several kills had one strong idea worth noting (Truck'N Roll's type scale, Pest Stop Boys' colour-blocked type, Clearabee's price cards), but the screener's two-tell rule killed them and I have not overridden it.
