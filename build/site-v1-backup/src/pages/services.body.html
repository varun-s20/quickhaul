What we take and what we don't

Most of what's in a house, a garage or a yard can go on the truck. A few things can't, for safety and because the dump won't take them from us. Not sure about something? Send a photo with your booking or call us and ask.

We take

  • Furniture of every size
  • Appliances
  • Mattresses and box springs
  • Boxes, bags and household junk
  • Yard waste
  • Demo debris
  • Electronics and TVs
  • Scrap wood and metal

We don't take

  • Paint, oil and chemicals
  • Propane tanks and gas cylinders
  • Asbestos
  • Medical waste
  • Car batteries
  • Anything still connected to gas

Priced by the load

You pay for how much of the truck your junk fills, and the price covers the loading, the hauling and the drop-off. Pick a size when you book and add photos, and we confirm the price before the day. Heavy loads like concrete, dirt or roofing shingles can cost more, so tell us about them up front.

Just one item? It's priced as a 1/8 load. Demo work is priced per job.
