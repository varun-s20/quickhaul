Still good? Someone wants it

A lot of what we pick up still works

The dresser that doesn't fit the new place, the kids' bikes, the spare couch from the basement. Throwing that in the dump makes no sense when someone nearby could use it.

So when we haul your junk, we sort out the good stuff and take it to local charities and reuse stores instead. You get the space back and the stuff gets a second life.

What we can donate

Furniture in good shape, working appliances, kitchenware, clothes and shoes, books, toys, bikes, tools and sports gear. If you'd use it, someone else probably will too.

What we can't

Anything broken, stained or missing parts, mattresses that have been slept on for years, car seats, recalled cribs and anything that's been in a flood or a fire.

Where it goes

Local charities and reuse stores in Massachusetts and New Hampshire that take household goods. What they accept changes, so we check before every drop-off.

How to ask

When you book, add a note saying which items are still good, or tell us on the day. We'll load them separately so they don't end up on the pile.
