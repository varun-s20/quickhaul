About Quick Haul

A local junk removal and demo crew, working towns across Massachusetts and New Hampshire.

Quick Haul is Shawn and a small crew who clear out garages, basements and yards across Massachusetts and New Hampshire, and take down the sheds and decks that need taking down.

Pick up the phone

Shawn started Quick Haul in 2022 and still answers the phone himself. That's on purpose. A two-minute call tells him what's in the garage, how to get the truck close and what to bring, and it tells you who's showing up and what it costs. You can book online any time, but if you'd rather talk it through first, call. That's what the number is for.

Every job is the same idea: show up when we said, carry out what you point at, leave the space clear and take the good stuff to donation where we can. No sales pitch, no guessing on the price.
